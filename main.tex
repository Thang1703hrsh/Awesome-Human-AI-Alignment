\section{Human--AI Alignment Taxonomy}
\label{sec:alignment-taxonomy}

\input{taxonomy}

Figure~\ref{fig:human-ai-alignment-taxonomy} presents our taxonomy of Human--AI Alignment. Rather than organizing the field by individual algorithms, we separate four questions that recur across the literature: \emph{what} the system should align to, \emph{how} that target is communicated as supervision, \emph{how} the resulting signal changes or controls model behavior, and \emph{how} the claimed alignment is evaluated and maintained. This organization synthesizes perspectives from general AI-alignment surveys, feedback-centered surveys, preference-learning surveys, personalized alignment, and multimodal alignment \citep{ji2025alignment,fernandes2024feedback,jiang2024preferencesurvey,gao2024unifiedpreference,guan2025personalizedsurvey,yu2025multimodalsurvey}.

\paragraph{Alignment specification.}
The first branch asks what constitutes desirable behavior and whose interests define that target. For \emph{alignment goals}, instruction- and task-following work ranges from FLAN, T0, and Natural/Super-NaturalInstructions to InstructGPT, Self-Instruct, LIMA, and the Flan Collection \citep{wei2022flan,sanh2022t0,mishra2022natural,wang2022superni,ouyang2022instructgpt,wang2023selfinstruct,zhou2023lima,longpre2023flancollection}. Reliable assistance extends beyond literal task completion to helpfulness, factuality, grounding, and response quality, as studied by general assistant models, WebGPT, fine-grained feedback, UltraFeedback, and HelpSteer2 \citep{askell2021assistant,bai2022hh,glaese2022sparrow,nakano2021webgpt,wu2023finegrained,cui2024ultrafeedback,wang2024helpsteer2}. Safety-oriented specifications explicitly encode harmlessness, refusal behavior, or safety constraints through approaches such as Constitutional AI, BeaverTails, Safe RLHF, rule-based rewards, and circuit-breaking interventions \citep{bai2022constitutional,ji2023beavertails,dai2024saferlhf,mu2024rulebased,zou2024circuitbreakers}. The \emph{alignment constituency} further distinguishes individual, collective, and institutional targets: personalized alignment adapts to user-specific preferences \citep{jang2023personalizedsoups,li2024personalized,poddar2024personalizing,zhang2025personajudge}, whereas pluralistic and collective alignment explicitly models disagreement across people, communities, or cultures \citep{bakker2022finetuning,siththaranjan2024distributional,huang2024collective,kirk2024prism,li2024culturellm,feng2024modular,sorensen2024pluralistic,adams2025steerable}. Finally, \emph{specification uncertainty} captures ambiguity, disagreement, and temporal drift. Earlier inverse-reward and cooperative formulations formalized uncertainty about human objectives \citep{hadfieldmenell2016cirl,hadfieldmenell2017ird,hadfieldmenell2017offswitch}, while recent work studies continual preference and safety adaptation as preferences or operating conditions evolve \citep{zhang2025copr,wang2025lifelong}.

\paragraph{Alignment supervision.}
The second branch characterizes how alignment requirements are translated into learning signals. Human supervision remains foundational, from preference-based reinforcement learning and early language-model preference tuning to summarization and instruction-following with human feedback \citep{christiano2017preferences,ziegler2019finetuning,stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instructgpt}. Model-generated supervision reduces reliance on direct human annotation and includes Constitutional AI, RLAIF, self-rewarding models, and direct alignment from AI-generated feedback \citep{bai2022constitutional,lee2024rlaif,yuan2024selfrewarding,liu2024dlma}. Programmatic supervision instead derives signals from rules, verifiable outcomes, or environment-level checks, as in rule-based safety rewards, mathematical verification, and RL with verifiable rewards \citep{mu2024rulebased,shao2024deepseekmath,deepseek2025r1}. Across these sources, feedback may be expressed as demonstrations, comparative or scalar evaluations, or natural-language critiques and explanations; critique-and-revision systems such as Self-Refine, Reflexion, and CRITIC illustrate the latter regime \citep{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion,gou2024critic}. The quality of supervision is itself an alignment problem: reward overoptimization, noisy or inconsistent annotations, active preference acquisition, and reward-model reliability motivate explicit feedback-quality analysis \citep{gao2023overoptimization,muldrew2024active,cheng2024rime,wang2024rmquality,lambert2025rewardbench}. Scalable oversight addresses cases in which humans cannot directly and reliably supervise stronger systems, motivating debate, amplification, weak-to-strong generalization, prover--verifier games, and control protocols \citep{irving2018debate,christiano2018amplification,burns2024weakstrong,khan2024debate,kirchner2024proververifier,greenblatt2024control}.

\paragraph{Alignment mechanisms.}
The third branch describes how supervision is converted into behavior. \emph{Feedback modeling} learns explicit reward models or verifiers from demonstrations and judgments; classic RLHF systems learn scalar reward functions \citep{christiano2017preferences,stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instructgpt}, whereas process supervision and verifier-based approaches assess intermediate reasoning or correctness \citep{uesato2022process,lightman2024verify,wang2024mathshepherd,kirchner2024proververifier,song2025prmbench,zhang2026biprm}. \emph{Policy learning} includes supervised instruction tuning \citep{wei2022flan,sanh2022t0,wang2022superni,zhou2023lima}, direct preference optimization and its major variants---RRHF, SLiC-HF, DPO, IPO/$\Psi$PO, KTO, ORPO, SimPO, TDPO, DNO, SPPO, and SDPO \citep{yuan2023rrhf,zhao2023slichf,rafailov2023dpo,azar2023psipo,ethayarajh2024kto,hong2024orpo,meng2024simpo,zeng2024tdpo,rosset2024dno,wu2025sppo,kong2025sdpo}---and reward-based reinforcement learning, including RLHF, RLAIF, safety-constrained RL, process-reward RL, and verifiable-reward training \citep{christiano2017preferences,stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instructgpt,dai2024saferlhf,lee2024rlaif,shao2024deepseekmath,deepseek2025r1}. Alignment knowledge can also be transferred through distillation, including CycleAlign, PLaD, DPKD, ADPA, PAD, and AlignDistil \citep{hong2024cyclealign,zhang2024plad,li2024dpkd,gao2025adpa,gu2025pad,zhang2025aligndistil}. Finally, \emph{inference control} modifies behavior without necessarily retraining the base policy, including representation-level intervention and activation steering \citep{li2023iti,rimsky2024caa}, critique/revision and search \citep{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion,gou2024critic}, and human-in-the-loop deferral, interruption, and control \citep{mozannar2020defer,orseau2016interruptible,greenblatt2024control}. These mechanisms also extend to multimodal systems through preference feedback, hallucination-aware alignment, and multimodal critics \citep{yu2024rlhfv,li2024vlfeedback,guan2024hallusionbench,xiong2025llavacritic}.

\paragraph{Alignment assurance.}
The final branch asks what evidence supports an alignment claim after training and during deployment. \emph{Behavioral evaluation} measures instruction following, helpfulness, factuality, safety, reasoning, and evaluator reliability using benchmarks and judge-based protocols such as TruthfulQA, FActScore, MT-Bench/Chatbot Arena, IFEval, XSTest, HarmBench, HallusionBench, RewardBench, and PRMBench \citep{lin2022truthfulqa,min2023factscore,zheng2023mtbench,zhou2023ifeval,chiang2024arena,rottger2024xstest,mazeika2024harmbench,guan2024hallusionbench,lambert2025rewardbench,song2025prmbench}. \emph{Robustness} asks whether alignment persists under attacks, distribution shift, and subsequent adaptation. Automated red teaming and jailbreak studies expose adversarial failures \citep{perez2022redteaming,zou2023jailbreak,mazeika2024harmbench,souly2024strongreject,zou2024circuitbreakers}; goal-misgeneralization work highlights failures under distributional shift \citep{langosco2022goal,shah2022goal}; and post-training studies show that otherwise aligned models can lose safety after further fine-tuning, motivating explicit alignment-preservation methods \citep{qi2024finetuning,zhang2025copr,wang2025lifelong}. Beyond behavior, \emph{system assurance} seeks deeper evidence through interpretability, verification, and monitoring. Latent-knowledge and representation-level analyses probe internal model states \citep{burns2023latent,li2023iti,rimsky2024caa}; verifier and process-supervision work provides structured evidence about intermediate reasoning \citep{uesato2022process,lightman2024verify,kirchner2024proververifier}; and broad evaluation, model-written tests, extreme-risk evaluation, AI-control protocols, and contamination analysis support ongoing auditing and deployment monitoring \citep{liang2023helm,perez2023modelwritten,shevlane2023extremerisks,greenblatt2024control,oren2024contamination}. Together, these four dimensions form a lifecycle view of alignment: specification determines the target, supervision communicates it, mechanisms realize it, and assurance tests whether it continues to hold.
